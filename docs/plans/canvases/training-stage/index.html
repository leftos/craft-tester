Where the training stage lives (CRAFT-57)

Each lane shows the same student on S1-OAK-2 Clean Clearances: home on top, then the strip page's toolbar with its Filters popover open. Things the stage rules out are shown but locked, with the module that unlocks them. Native dropdowns can't be captured open, so their option lists are drawn below them.

The stage is the first choice on home, above Airport, because it decides what the rows below offer. On a strip it sits at the top of the Filters popover.
